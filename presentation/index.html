Here's a secret

Computers follow instructions

Just like a recipe! 🍳

1
🥚 Get eggs from the fridge
2
🔥 Turn on the stove
3
🍽️ Cook and serve!
🤖

That's it — that's how every program works.

Ask: "What happens if you forget step 2 and skip turning on the stove?" — (the eggs stay raw!)

Computers are the same: wrong order = wrong result. That's why programmers think very carefully about the ORDER of instructions.

Fun follow-up: "Who's written instructions for someone before? Like directions to your house?"
